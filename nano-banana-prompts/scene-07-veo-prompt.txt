VEO 3 Prompt — Scene 7: Battery Pack Animation

Generation Prompt
Starting and ending on an identical still frame — an elevated three-quarter view of an open EV battery pack, rows of cylindrical lithium-ion cells in a brushed-metal housing against a pure black background — animate a single continuous charging wave across the cell array: warm orange (#E8601C) emissive glow on each cell rises slowly from near-black to full luminance and falls back to near-black in a rolling wave that originates from the rear-left corner of the pack, travels diagonally across the cell rows toward the front-right edge, and completes one full pass in exactly 6–7 seconds so the final frame matches the opening frame with no visible cut; the wave motion is smooth and sinusoidal, not flickering or stroboscopic; simultaneously, execute an imperceptible 4–5% dolly-in on the camera — starting slightly wider and ending fractionally closer — timed so the push begins and resolves gently within the same window, invisible unless frames are compared directly; the pack housing, overhead key light, hard shadow geometry, brushed-metal specular highlights, and background remain completely static throughout; no secondary animation, no particle movement, no lens flare pulse, no new objects; the only perceptible change in the scene is the traveling orange luminance wave moving across the cell tops — everything else holds perfectly still.

What success looks like
A hypnotic, near-frozen shot where the only thing alive is a slow orange wave rolling silently through the cells — the camera barely breathes, the housing never moves, and the loop is invisible.
